Flexible financing, made simple

The Motorcoach Store is ready to guide you through the finance process for your RV purchase. Our team has established relationships with the top lenders who specialize in Prevost, Newell and Newmar financing along with the other major brands in the Class A, Class B and Class C markets.

With terms up to 20 years and low fixed rates, monthly payment options can be tailored to your desired budget with the cash down payment you like best. The industry standard down payment is 20%, but we can help with lower down payments depending on both your qualifications and the sales price of the RV. We'll outline everything you need to know.
