Ultimate Protection for Your Investment

Your motorcoach deserves to turn heads on — and off — the road. Our professional ceramic coating locks in that showroom-fresh shine and shields your coach from UV rays, acid rain, bugs, and road grime for years. Unlike waxes or sealants, our coating bonds directly with your paint for durable protection, lasting luster, and long-term value.

Why Ceramic Coating?
  • Superior Protection – Shields against UV rays, oxidation, acid rain, bug residue, and road grime.
  • Effortless Maintenance – Water, dirt, and debris bead and slide off, making cleaning faster and easier.
  • Extended Longevity – Preserves your finish for years, reducing the need for constant waxing.
  • Showroom Gloss – Amplifies your coach’s color and delivers a deep, mirror-like shine.
  • Value Preservation – Keeps your motor coach looking its best, protecting resale value.

Ceramic Coatings Explained

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: How long does ceramic coating last?

A: With proper maintenance, many coatings last 3–5 years.

Q: Do I still need to wash my motor coach?

A: Yes. Ceramic coating makes washing easier, but it does not eliminate the need for cleaning. Dirt and contaminants will not bond as strongly, so washing takes less time and effort.

Q: Can ceramic coating be applied to any motor coach?

 A: Yes. Whether new or pre-owned, ceramic coating enhances and protects all finishes. On older coaches, we often perform paint correction first for the best results.

Q: Will ceramic coating protect against scratches or rock chips?

A: Ceramic coating provides a durable layer of protection, but it is not bulletproof. It resists light swirls and micro-scratches but does not replace clear bra/paint protection film for impact resistance.

Common Myths About Ceramic Coating

Myth 1: Ceramic coating makes a coach scratch-proof.

Reality: It reduces minor scratches and swirl marks but cannot prevent deeper scratches or rock chips.

Myth 2: Ceramic coating means you never have to wash again.

Reality: You still need to wash your coach, but the process is much easier, faster, and less frequent.

Myth 3: Ceramic coating is just like waxing.

Reality: Wax lasts a few months, while ceramic coating chemically bonds to your paint and can last years.

Myth 4: Any detail shop can apply it the same way.

Reality: Proper surface preparation and expert application are critical. At The Motorcoach Store, our trained technicians specialize in treating large, luxury motor coaches with precision.
