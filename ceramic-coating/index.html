CERAMIC COATING

Protect. Preserve. Perfect.

Ultimate Protection

A ceramic coating for the finish, applied by the Bradenton detail team, meant to keep a showroom gloss and shed water, dirt, and road film.

Why Ceramic

It is not a substitute for washing, and it does not make paint scratch-proof or chip-proof. It makes maintenance washes easier and helps the finish hold its look longer.

Certified Technicians

Coating work is handled by the same shop that prepares coaches for delivery, with paint correction available when an older finish needs it first.

Proven Process

  1. Wash and decontamination
  2. Paint correction where the finish needs it
  3. Coating application
  4. Cure
  5. Final inspection

Why Choose The Motorcoach Store

The coating is applied in the same Bradenton facility as service and paint, so a coach already in for other work can be scheduled without a separate trip to an outside detailer.

FAQ

How long does it last?

With maintenance, many coatings last 3 to 5 years.

Do I still wash the coach?

Yes. Dirt still has to come off. The coating makes that wash easier. It is not wax, and it does not end washing.

Can it go on an older coach?

Yes. Older finishes often get paint correction first so the coating is not locking in swirls.

Will it stop rock chips?

No. Rock chips need paint and body work. Coating protects the finish that is already there.

Myths

Coating is not armor, not a polish that lasts forever, and not a reason to skip washing. It is a protective layer on a prepared finish.
