Introducing Our Certified Pre-Owned Program

Buying a pre-owned motorcoach shouldn’t mean compromising on quality or peace of mind. Our Certified Pre-Owned (CPO) Program gives you near-new assurance, with rigorous inspections, a limited warranty, and lifetime ownership benefits.

What Criteria Must A Coach Meet To Become The Motorcoach Store Certified?

We only certify the finest coaches and only those that meet strict eligibility requirements designed to ensure quality, reliability, and long-term value.

To qualify for The Motorcoach Store Certified Pre-Owned Program, a motor coach must meet the following criteria:

  • It must be no more than five years old, based on the current model year.
  • It must have 50,000 miles or less.
  • It must hold a clean title, with no history of salvage, flood, or branding.
  • It must show no evidence of major accident damage or structural repairs.
  • It must be free of pre-existing mechanical or cosmetic issues at the time of delivery.
  • It must successfully pass a comprehensive 400-point inspection; completed by The Motorcoach Store’s certified service technicians.
  • All major systems and components must be fully functional and operational at the time of delivery.
  • A certified dealership technician must complete and sign off on the unit’s final certification documentation.

Only motor coaches that meet all requirements earn the Certified Pre-Owned designation — a reflection of the quality and confidence that The Motorcoach Store stands behind.

What Do We Do To Certify A Coach?

Once a unit qualifies for certification, it goes through a detailed process to ensure it meets the full standard of The Motorcoach Store’s Certified Pre-Owned Program.

Here’s what we do:

  • Our 400-point inspection covers every major system in the coach — from chassis, powertrain, generators, drivetrain to appliances, HVAC, plumbing, and freshwater systems.
  • Every component covered under warranty is tested for full functionality, and any deficiencies are noted.
  • Our technicians repair or replace any non-compliant parts to bring the unit up to certification standards.
  • A certified inspection checklist is completed and signed by a trained dealership technician.
  • The unit is officially designated as Certified Pre-Owned and clearly labeled as such on the lot and online.

This process ensures that every certified motor coach offers reliability and peace of mind from day one.

Certified Pre-Owned Coverage Explained

Engine, Transmission & Drivetrain

Engine, Transmission & Drivetrain

  • Pistons, rings, bearings, crankshaft, camshaft, and valves
  • Timing components
  • Water/fuel/oil pumps
  • Turbocharger
  • Diesel injectors/heater
  • Internal transmission components, torque converter, Modulator
  • Cooler, shift control unit, mounts & Pan
  • Differential housing, CV joints, U-joints, and bearings
  • axle shafts, driveshafts, and supports

 

Brakes

  • Master cylinder
  • Electric/hydraulic brakes
  • Actuators

Suspension

  • Spindles
  • Hub bearings and wheel bearings

Frame

  • Metal frame
  • Brackets and welds

Power & Generator Systems

  • Internal engine components, block, and cylinder head
  • Starter, ignition coil, and voltage regulator
  • Generator assembly

Climate & Comfort Systems

Air conditioning systems:

  • compressor, evaporator, and condenser
  • Accumulator, Expansion valve and Receiver drier

Heating systems:

  • Ignitor/ignition assembly
  • Burner elements, thermocouple, and gas valve
  • Thermostat and Blower/fan motors

Appliances & Kitchen Components

Refrigerator & Freezer

  • Thermostat, Thermocouple, and cooling unit
  • Burner assembly, igniter, and control panels
  • Switches and Printed circuit boards

Range & Oven

  • Power hood, and range burner assembly
  • Thermostat, and thermocouple
  • Burner valves and igniter/ignition assembly

Water & Sanitation Systems

Fresh Water System

  • Water pump, compressor, and water tanks
  • Manifold and faucets

Sanitation & Waste System

  • Shower basin, toilet, and Sink(s)
  • In-sink disposal & switch
  • Holding tanks and gate valves

Water Heater

  • Thermocouple
  • Gas valve
  • Ignition assembly

LP Gas Systems

Regulators, gas bottles (except valves/gauges)

Mounting brackets, pigtails, and gas lines

Connections, Leak detector and auto shut-off valve

  • Free annual engine oil change for the lifetime of coach ownership, provided at The Motorcoach Store.
