Making Your Dreams a Reality

A Bradenton showroom for new and pre-owned luxury motor coaches, with service and paint under the same roof.

Our Story & Mission

The Motorcoach Store was founded in Bradenton, Florida to sell a focused collection of luxury motor coaches and back them with service. The showroom is built around coaches you can compare indoors.

Meet the Founder & CEO

Bradley Twait owns the company and is the person the showroom points to for the standard a coach has to meet before it is offered. The stated aim is a coach that works as a home on the road.

You’re Our Guest

  • Quality over quantity: find the coach that fits, rather than pushing the one that is easiest to sell.
  • Satisfaction is checked after the choice, not only at the signature.
  • Repeat service, paint, and the next coach are part of the same relationship.

Unique Showroom & Services

The indoor showroom holds new and pre-owned coaches from lines such as Prevost, Newell, and Newmar, plus Loki, OGV, ShowHauler, and Featherlite listings. Next to sales, the building includes a service center, a body and paint shop, and purchasing for Class A coaches.
