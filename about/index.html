Meet the founder & CEO

Our owner, Bradley Twait, personally ensures that every aspect of our business meets the highest standards of quality. When most entrepreneurs retreated during the recession, Bradley Twait accelerated, launching The Motorcoach Store in 2010 with a bold belief that affluent buyers hadn’t disappeared, they were simply waiting for the right opportunity. What followed was a complete reinvention of the luxury RV buying experience: no-pressure “hosts” instead of salespeople, transparent pricing instead of negotiation games, and a relentless focus on guest experience over transactions. In this exclusive interview, Twait shares how conviction during uncertainty helped build one of the country’s leading luxury motorcoach businesses, and why today’s wealthy buyers are redefining freedom, choosing lifestyle, flexibility, and experience over traditional status symbols like second homes.
